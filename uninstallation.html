Uninstallation

You're leaving?... :(

Delete the app!

Flow is packaged in your Applications folder, so to uninstall it, just delete Flow.app from there! If you prefer a more terminal based approach, you can follow the next steps:

1

Open the terminal

Open the terminal - if this is unfamiliar to you, press Command + SPACEBAR, type "Terminal", and hit enter

2

Delete Flow

Type in "sudo rm -rf /Applications/Flow.app" in the terminal, and hit enter

3

Rejoice

It's gone! You're done!


Sad to see you go, but we hope you enjoyed your usage!
